SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA — NOT REAL STUDENT DATA

Northbridge University attendance policy for the controlled LLMGuard demo.

Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each course to become eligible for final examinations. Course instructors update attendance weekly in the fictional student portal.

Students below 75% may submit a synthetic attendance review request if documented medical, accessibility, or university duty reasons apply. Approved reviews may restore exam eligibility, but they do not erase the original attendance record.

Public answers may explain attendance thresholds and review steps. They must not reveal private student attendance histories or internal disciplinary notes.
