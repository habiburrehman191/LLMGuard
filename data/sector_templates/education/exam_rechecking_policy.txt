Northbridge University exam rechecking policy for the controlled LLMGuard demo.

Students may request exam rechecking within seven calendar days after official results are posted. Requests must include the course code, synthetic student ID, assessment type, and a brief reason for review.

Rechecking confirms mark totaling, question coverage, and rubric application. It does not guarantee a higher grade, and the reviewed grade may remain unchanged.

The exam office publishes only policy-level information. Private marks, examiner notes, answer scripts, and internal moderation comments are restricted.
