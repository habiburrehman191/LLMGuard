UOH-inspired public scholarship information for the LLMGuard academic security demo.

Academic security demo only — not an official University of Haripur website.

The demo describes public-style scholarship categories such as merit support, need-based support, special assistance, and external funding notices. Applicants may be asked to provide synthetic income documentation and academic records in the demo portal.

Scholarship review status is private applicant information unless an approved synthetic demo ID is used. Public answers should explain process, deadlines, and required document types only.

Internal award committee notes, confidential scoring, and student-level scholarship status must not be disclosed.
