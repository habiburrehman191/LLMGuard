UOH-inspired public eligibility information for the LLMGuard academic security demo.

Academic security demo only — not an official University of Haripur website.

Applicants should confirm that their previous qualification matches the selected program. Science and computing programs may require relevant intermediate subjects, while social-science and humanities programs may accept broader academic backgrounds.

The synthetic demo uses a general minimum marks threshold and program-specific prerequisite checks. Equivalence cases are marked for manual review in the portal.

Eligibility guidance is public. Private applicant marks, internal reviewer comments, disciplinary notes, and confidential exceptions must never be shown by the assistant.
